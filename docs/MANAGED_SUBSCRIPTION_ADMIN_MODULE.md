# Managed subscriptions: CUP module and analytics contract

## Scope

The CUP module has three top-level sections. This document defines their server
contracts and business semantics; it does not add the CUP screen to this LK
repository and does not publish or activate a real Viva product.

The TypeScript response mirror for LK/new-cabinet adapters is
`src/types/managedSubscriptionAdmin.ts`.

## 1. Subscription settings

The settings section edits a new immutable DRAFT policy version. Publishing is a
separate audited action. Existing client instances keep their pinned policy
version unless an administrator runs an explicit impact preview and migration.

The editor contains appendable rows for:

- create/join actions and allowed durations;
- active-services limit: enabled switch, maximum and counter scope;
- booking window: enabled switch and number of station-local calendar days;
- station access: home station, explicit station checkboxes or all stations,
  with a fixed surcharge per row and explicit priority;
- benefits: action, event category, event type IDs, optional add-on product type
  IDs, durations, station checkboxes and benefit kind;
- lifecycle: validity, freeze, cancellation and no-show consequences;
- usage: daily/weekly/monthly limits, future bookings, blackout dates and minimum
  interval between uses.

Overlapping station or benefit rows with the same priority are invalid. The
server must resolve all IDs and prices; the browser only sends selected canonical
IDs from server-provided dictionaries.

## 2. Subscription management

The list is instance-based, not just product-based. Minimum columns and filters:

- client, masked phone, home station and subscription type;
- Viva catalogue `productId`, local instance ID and provider instance evidence;
- state, purchase/activation/expiry dates and pinned policy version;
- visits used/remaining, active bookings and last visit;
- paid amount, discounts, refunds and renewal sequence;
- renewal state: due soon, renewed, 7/14/21/30 days overdue, cancelled/refunded;
- actions with RBAC and audit trail: inspect, freeze/unfreeze, migrate policy,
  revoke and export. Financial/provider mutations require a separate confirmed
  provider contract and exact read-back.

The overdue cohorts are mutually exclusive by `daysSinceExpiry`: `7–13`,
`14–20`, `21–29`, and `30+`. The UI may additionally show `1–6` as an early
follow-up queue. A client is removed from all overdue cohorts as soon as a paid
renewal is authoritatively linked to the expired instance.

## 3. Subscription analytics

Every card uses the same selected period, station/type filters and comparison
period of equal duration immediately preceding it. Responses include `asOf`,
time zone, numerator, denominator and data freshness; an unavailable source is
shown as unavailable rather than zero.

Core KPIs:

1. active subscriptions at period end;
2. subscriptions bought in the period, split into new clients and renewals;
3. renewal rate and non-renewed cohorts with drill-down lists for 7/14/21/30+;
4. gross sales, refunds, net subscription revenue and average purchase value;
5. average and median attended visits per active subscription;
6. utilization distribution: zero-use, low-use, normal-use and heavy-use;
7. cancellation and confirmed no-show rate;
8. discount cost and add-on revenue generated by subscription holders;
9. booking conversion: eligible decision → reservation → paid/confirmed visit;
10. station and time-of-day slot occupancy, split by subscribers/non-subscribers;
11. renewal revenue and cohort retention by purchase month, station and tariff;
12. outstanding entitlement liability: paid visits/units not yet consumed.

### Capacity and overdraft indicators

- `eligibleSlotCapacity`: bookable participant slots for event types and stations
  covered by the policy in the selected period;
- `subscriberOccupiedSlots`: confirmed attended or future-reserved subscriber
  slots in that capacity;
- `subscriberSlotFillRate = subscriberOccupiedSlots / eligibleSlotCapacity`;
- `unusedEligibleCapacity = eligibleSlotCapacity - allConfirmedOccupiedSlots`;
- `entitlementDemand`: remaining subscription units that can become bookings in
  the configured booking window;
- `capacityOverdraftUnits = max(0, entitlementDemand - unusedEligibleCapacity)`.

`capacityOverdraftUnits` is a planning-risk indicator, not money owed and not a
promise that every outstanding annual entitlement will be consumed at once. It
must be calculated by station, event type and peak/off-peak interval; one global
number can hide a local evening shortage.

### Required event ledger

Analytics must be built from an append-only, idempotent ledger rather than the
current mutable subscription row. Minimum events: purchase initiated/paid/
failed/refunded, instance activated/frozen/expired/revoked, eligible decision,
reservation created/released, booking confirmed/cancelled, attendance consumed/
returned, no-show confirmed and renewal linked. Each event stores correlation
ID, canonical station/event/product IDs, policy version, money in minor units,
provider evidence reference and occurred/recorded timestamps.

PII drill-down lists require subscription-operations RBAC and must not be part of
aggregate analytics exports by default.
